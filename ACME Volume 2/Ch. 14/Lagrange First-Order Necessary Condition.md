###### Assumptions
$\Omega,\ f: \Omega \to \mathbb R,\ H: \Omega \to \mathbb R^{\mathscr l},\ \mathscr F$ define the [[Non-Linear Constrained Minimization Problem]] in standard form
$x^*$ is a local minimizer of ($f, H$)
$x^*$ is a [[Regular Point]]
$\lambda^* \in \mathbb R^{\mathscr l}$ 
$D$ is the [[Derivative]]
###### Conclusions
$\lambda^*$ exists such that
$$
Df(x^*) + (\lambda^*)^T DH(x^*) = 0
$$
