#Definition Definition 13.1.4
## Intuition
Taking a set and adding just enough to it to make it convex

###### Assumptions
$\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_k \in [0,1]$
$\lambda_1 + \dots + \lambda_k = 1$

## Key Results
- the convex hull is convex (Proposition 13.1.5)
- the convex hull of a convex set is itself (Proposition 13.1.6)
