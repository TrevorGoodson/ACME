## Technical Details
###### Assumption
$V$ is a [[Inner Product Space]]
$\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle$ is an [[Inner Product]] on $V$
$S \subset V$ is the candidate for a half space
$a \in V$ is not the [[Zero Vector]]
$b \in \mathbb R$
###### Definition
$S$ is defined as a half space if it can be written as follows for some $a,b$
$$
S = \{ x \in V \mid \langle a,x \rangle \le b \}
$$
The [[Hyperplane]] defined as $S = \{ x \in V \mid \langle a,x \rangle \le b \}$
