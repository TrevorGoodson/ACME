# Affine Function
#Definition Definition 13.1.12
## Intuition
## Technical Details
###### Assumptions
$V,W$ are [[Vector Space]]s
$f:V \to W$ is the candidate for being affine
$L:V \to W$ is a [[Linear Transformation]]
$c \in W$ and $x \in V$

###### Definition
$f$ is defined as affine if it can be written as follows for some $L$ and all $x$
$$
f(x) = L(x) + c
$$
