#Definition Definition 13.1.16
## Intuition
$a$ sets the direction; everything in the hyperplane is [[ACME Volume 1/Ch. 3 Inner Product Spaces/Orthogonal|Orthogonal]] to it.
$b$ is the "offset"

## Technical Details
$V$ is an [[Inner Product Space]]
$a \in V$ is not the [[Zero Vector]]
$b \in \mathbb R$

###### Definition
Set $a,b$. For any $x$.
$$
H = \{ x \in V \mid \langle a,x \rangle = b \}
$$

## Key Results
- a hyper plane is an [[Affine]] set
