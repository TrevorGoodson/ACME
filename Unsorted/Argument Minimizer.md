## Technical Details
###### Assumptions
$X$ is any set
$f:X \to \mathbb R$
$Y \subset \mathbb R$ is the [[Image]] of $f$
$x,y \in X$
$\min$ is the [[Minimum]]
###### Definition
The argument minimizer satisfies 
$\underset{X}{\text{argmin}}\ f(x) = y$ such that $f(y) = \min(Y)$
