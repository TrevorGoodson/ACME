## Intuition
It's a complex number where the imaginary part is multiplied by $-1$. Another way to think about it is the number flipped across the real number line.
## Technical Details
$c \in \mathbb C$, where $c$ can be written as $c = a + bi$, where $a,b \in \mathbb R$. The complex conjugate of $c$, denoted $\bar c$, is defined as:
$$
\bar c = a - bi
$$
You can also take the complex conjugate of a matrix (let's call it $A$) too, denoted $\bar A$, where you take the complex conjugate of each element.
