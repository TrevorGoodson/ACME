#Definition Definition 1.3.2
# Technical Definition
###### Assumptions
$A \subset \mathbb R$
$s, b \in \mathbb R$
$s, b$ are [[Bounded|upper bounds]] for $A$
$X$ is any set
$f: X \to A$
$x \in X$
###### Definition
$s$ is the supremum of $A$, denoted $\sup (A)$ if $s \le b$ for any $b$.
To denote the supremum of the image of some function $f$, you can use the following notation:
$$
\sup_{x \in X}f(x)
$$
