# Intuition
The derivative is bounded by a constant

# Technical Definition
###### Symbols
$K > 0$
$X, Y$ are [[Metric|metric]] spaces
$x_1, x_2 \in X$
$f: X \to Y$
$d_X$ is the distance function on $X$
$d_Y$ is the distance function on $Y$

###### Definition
$f$ is Lipschitz continuous of $\exists K > 0$ such that
$$ d_Y(f(x_1), f(x_2)) \le K d_X(x_1, x_2) $$
for all $x_1, x_2$
