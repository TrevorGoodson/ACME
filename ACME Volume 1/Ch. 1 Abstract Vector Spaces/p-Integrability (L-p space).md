#Definition  Example 1.1.6 (iv)

## Technical Definition
###### Declarations
$p \in \mathbb R$ such that $p \ge 1$ or $p = \infty$

###### Definition
$$
L^p([a,b]; \mathbb F) = \left\{f:[a,b] \to \mathbb F \ \ \middle\vert \ \ \int_a^b |f(x)|^p dx < \infty \right\}
$$

for $p= \infty$
$$
L^{\infty} ([a,b]; \mathbb F) = \left\{f:[a,b] \to \mathbb F \ \ \middle\vert \ \ \sup_{x \in [a,b]}|f(x)| < \infty \right\}
$$
## Key Results
- $L^p, L^{\infty}$ are [[Vector Space|vector spaces]] (Example 1.1.6 (iv))
- $L^{\infty} \subset \cdots \subset L^p \subset \cdots \subset L^1$ on finite dimensional domains (source was AI, so maybe?)
