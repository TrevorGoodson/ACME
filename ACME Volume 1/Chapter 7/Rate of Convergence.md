
# Technical Definition
###### Symbols
$(x_n)_{n=0}^{\infty} \to \bar x$ is a sequence 
$\epsilon_n = ||x_n - \bar x||$ is called the error of the sequence at $x_n$
$n \in \mathbb N$
$\mu, k \in \mathbb R$

# Linear Convergence
If $\exists \mu \lt 1$ such that
$$\epsilon_{n+1} \le \mu \epsilon_n$$
for all $n$

# Quadratic Convergence
If $\exists k \ge 0$ such that
$$ \epsilon_{n+1} \le k \epsilon_n^2 $$
for all $n$
