#Definition Definition 4.1.9
## Intuition
$\text{det}(zI - A)$

## Technical Details
###### Assumptions
$z \in \mathbb F$
$A \in M_n(\mathbb F)$
$\text{det}(\cdot)$ is the [[Determinant]]
###### Definition
The characteristic polynomial of $A$, denoted $p_A(z)$ or $p(z)$, is defined to be:
$$
p_A(z) = \det(zI-A)
$$
## Key Results
#### Connection to eigenvalues
The roots of $p_A(z)$ are the [[Eigenvalue|eigenvalues]] of $A$ (Remark 4.1.10)
