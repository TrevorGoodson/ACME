#Definition Definition 4.4.3
## Intuition

## Technical Details
###### Assumptions
$A \in M_n(\mathbb F)$
$( \cdot )^H$ is the [[Hermitian]]

###### Definition
$A$ is defined to be normal if
$$
A^HA = AA^H
$$

## Key Results
- $A$ is normal if and only if it is orthonormally diagonalizable (Second Spectral Theorem, Theorem 4.4.14)
- Hermitian matrices are normal (Example 4.4.13)
