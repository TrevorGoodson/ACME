# Hermitian Operator
#Definition (Can't find it in anywhere in the textbooks)
$A$ is a matrix
$\bar A$ is the [[Complex Conjugate]] of $A$
$(\cdot)^T$ is the [[Transpose]]
The Hermitian of $A$, denoted $A^H$, is defined as 
$$
A^H = (\bar A)^T
$$


# Hermitian Matrix
#Definition Section prelude 4.4
A Hermitian matrix satisfies
$$
A = A^H
$$

## Key Results
- If $A$ is Hermitian, then it has an orthonormal eigenbasis and its eigenvalues are real (Corollary 4.4.9)
- Hermitian matrices are orthonormally diagonalizable; orthonormally similar to a diagonal matrix. (First Spectral Theorem, Theorem 4.4.7)
