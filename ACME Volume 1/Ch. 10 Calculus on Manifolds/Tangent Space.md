## Intuition

## Technical Details
###### Assumptions
$X$ is a [[Banach Space]]
$M \subset X$
$U \in \mathbb R^m$ is  [[Open]]
$\alpha : U \to M$ is a [[Parameterized Manifold]]
$u \in U$
$\alpha(u) = p$
$\mathscr R(\cdot)$ is the [[Image]]
$D$ is the [[Derivative]]
###### Definition
The tangent space of $M$ at $p$, denoted $T_p M$, is defined as:
$$
T_p M = \mathscr R(D\alpha(u))
$$

## Key Results
- The tangent space of a manifold is independent of of parameterization and orientation (Proposition 10.3.8)
- The tangent space is a [[Vector Space]]
- The tangent space is a [[Hyperplane]] if its dimension is one less than $X$.
