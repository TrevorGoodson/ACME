#Definition Definition 10.3.1
## Intuition
A generalization of a parameterized curve in higher dimensions

## Technical Details
###### Assumptions
$X$ is a [[Banach Space]]
$U \in \mathbb R^m$ is [[Open]]
$u \in U$
$\alpha \in C^1(U;X)$ ([[Continuously Differentiable]]) is the candidate for being a parameterized manafold
$D$ is the [[Derivative]]
###### Definition
$\alpha$ is defined to be a parameterized manifold if two conditions are met:
- It is [[Injective]]
- $D\alpha(u)$ is also injective for every $u$
