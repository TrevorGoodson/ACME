The derivative is not a single definition, but a set of definitions that all seek to define how a function changes at a certain input point. There is a separate definition for the derivative depending on the domain and codomain.

---

Let $X, Y$ be sets with $f:X \to Y$

If $X,Y$ are [[Banach Space|Banach spaces]], then the derivative is denoted $Df$, and is called the [[Fréchet Derivative]].

If $X,Y$ are subsets of $\mathbb R$, then the derivative is denoted $\frac{d}{dx} f$ or $f'$ and is called the [[Scalar Derivative]].

If $X$ is multidimensional, and the derivative is taken in a direction, then it is referring to the [[Directional Derivative]].

If $f$ is a function of multiple variables, and the derivative is taken only in terms of a single variable, then this is the [[Directional Derivative]].
