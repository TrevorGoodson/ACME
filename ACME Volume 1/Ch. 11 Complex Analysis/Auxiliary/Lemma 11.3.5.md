#Theorem Lemma 11.3.5
## Intuition
A simple integral around a singularity evaluates to 1 or 0.

## Technical Details
###### Assumptions
$\int \cdot\ d\cdot$ is the [[Integral]]
$z_0 \in \mathbb C$
$\gamma$ is a [[Contour|simple closed contour]] surrounding $z_0$
###### Conclusions
$$
\frac1{2\pi i} \int_{\gamma} (z-z_0)^n\ dz = \left\{ \begin{array}{rcl} 1 & \text{if } n=-1 \\ 0 & \text{else} \end{array} \right.
$$
