#Theorem Theorem 11.3.11
## Intuition
An integral over a loop in $\mathbb C$ of a holomorphic function evaluates to zero.

## Technical Details
###### Assumptions
$X \subset \mathbb C$
$U \subset \mathbb C$ is [[Simply Connected]]
$\Gamma \subset U$ is a [[Contour|simple closed contour]]
$z \in U$
$f:U \to X$ is [[Holomorphic]]
###### Conclusions
$$
\int_{\Gamma} f(z)dz = 0
$$
