#Definition 
## Intuition
A special definition of a [[Derivative]] for a complex function

## Technical Details
###### Assumptions
$\lim$ is the [[Limit]]
$U \in \mathbb C$ is [[Open]]
$z, z_0 \in U$
$f: U \to \mathbb C$
###### Definition
$f$ is defined to be holomorphic if the following limit exists for all $z$:
$$
\lim_{z \to z_0} \frac{f(z) - f(z_0)}{z - z_0}
$$
