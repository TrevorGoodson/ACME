#Definition Definition 11.1.1
## Intuition
A special definition of a [[Derivative]] for a complex function

## Technical Details
###### Assumptions
$\lim$ is the [[Limit]]
$U \in \mathbb C$ is [[Open]]
$z, z_0, h \in U$
$f: U \to \mathbb C$
###### Definition
$f$ is defined to be holomorphic if the following limit exists for all $z_0$:
$$
\lim_{z \to z_0} \frac{f(z) - f(z_0)}{z - z_0}\ =\ \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(z_0 + h) - f(z_0)}{h}
$$
