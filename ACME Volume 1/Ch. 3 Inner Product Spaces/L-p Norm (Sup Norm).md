#Definition Example 3.5.7

[[p-Integrability (L-p space)]]

$$
\| f \|_{L^p} = \left( \ \int_a^b |f|^p dx \ \right)^{1/p}
$$

$$
\| f \|_{L^{\infty}} = \sup_{x \in [a,b]} |f(x)|
$$
$(L^{\infty} ([a, b], X), \| \cdot \|_{L^{\infty}})$ is a [[Norm|normed]] [[Linearity|linear]] space & contains all [[Boundedness|bounded]] functions on $[a,b]$
