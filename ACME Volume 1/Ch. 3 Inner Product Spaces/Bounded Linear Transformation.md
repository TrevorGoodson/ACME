#Definition Definition 3.5.10

## Technical Definition
###### Declarations

###### Definitions
The set of bounded linear transformations, denoted $\mathscr B(V;W)$, is defined by the ser of [[Linearity|linear]] maps where the following quantity is [[Boundedness|bounded]]:
$$
\| T \|_{V,W} = \sup_{x \ne 0} \frac{\|T(x)\|_W}{\|x\|_V} = \sup_{\|x\|_V = 1} \|T(x)\|_W
$$
$\| \cdot \|_{V,W}$  is called the induced norm on $\mathscr B(V;W)$ 

## Key Results
- ($\mathscr B(V;W), \| \cdot \|_{V,W})$ is indeed a [[Norm|normed]] [[Linearity|linear]] space (Theorem 3.5.11)
