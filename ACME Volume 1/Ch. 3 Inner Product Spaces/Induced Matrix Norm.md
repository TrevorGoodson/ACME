#Definition Example 3.5.17

###### Declarations
$A \in M_{m \times n}(\mathbb F)$
$\mathbf 0, x \in \mathbb F^m$
$\| \cdot \|_p$ is a [[p-norm]] on both $\mathbb F^n$ and $\mathbb F^M$
$\sup$ is the [[Supremum]]

###### Definition
$\| A \|_p$ is the induced matrix norm of $A$ if it satisfies:
$$
\|A\|_p = \sup_{x \ne \mathbf 0} \frac{\|Ax\|_p}{\|x\|_p}
$$
