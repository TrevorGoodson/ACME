## Technical Details
###### Assumptions
$V$ is a [[Vector Space]]
$X \subset V$ is a [[Subspace]]
$\{x_i\}_{i=1}^m$ is an [[Orthonormal]] basis for $X$
$v \in V$
$\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle$ is an [[Inner Product]]

###### Definition
For any $v$, the projection of $v$ onto $X$ is defined to be
$$
\text{proj}_X(v) = \sum_{i=1}^m \langle x_i, v \rangle x_i
$$

## Key Results
- An orthogonal projection is a [[Projection]]
- An orthogonal projection is a [[Linear Transformation|linear operator]] (Theorem 3.2.7)
