#Definition Example 3.5.5
A specific example of a [[Norm]]
not to be confused with [[L-p Norm (Sup Norm)]]
## Technical Details
$p \in \mathbb N$
$x$ is a vector (each element accessed by $x_i$)
$$
\|x\|_p = \left( \sum_{j=1}^{n} |x_j|^p \right)^{1/p}
$$

If we think of $p = \infty$, we can also include this in the p-norms:
$$
\|x\|_{\infty} = \lim_{p \to \infty} \|x\|_p
$$
$\lim$ is the [[Limit]]
