## Technical Details
###### Assumptions
$A \in M_n(\mathbb F)$
$\rho(A)$ is the [[Resolvent Set]]
$z \in \rho(A)$
$(\cdot)^{-1}$ is the [[Inverse]]

###### Definition
The resolvent of $A$, denoted $R(A, \cdot)$, is a map $\rho(A) \to M_n(\mathbb C)$ given by
$$
R(A,z) = (zI-A)^{-1}
$$
