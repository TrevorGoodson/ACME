## Technical Details
###### Assumptions
$A \in M_n(\mathbb C)$
$\sigma(A)$ is the [[Spectrum]]
$\lambda \in \sigma(A)$ (an [[Eigenvalue]] of $A$)
$R(z)$ is the [[Resolvent]] of $A$
$A_k$ is the $k$th coefficient of the [[Laurent Expansion]] of $R(z)$ around $\lambda$
###### Definition
The eigennilpotent of $A$ with regards to $\lambda$, denoted $D_{\lambda}$ is defined as
$$
D_{\lambda} = A_{-2}
$$
## Key Results
#### Relationship to Eigenprojection
$$
D_{\lambda} = (A - \lambda I)P_{\lambda}
$$
([[Spectral Projection (Eigenprojection)]]) (Lemma 12.6.1)
#### Spectral Radius
The [[Spectral Radius]] of $D_{\lambda}$ is zero. (Lemma 12.6.1)
