## Technical Details
###### Assumptions
$A \in M_n(\mathbb F)$
$I$ is the identity matrix
$z \in \mathbb C$
$(\cdot)^{-1}$ is the inverse
###### Definition
The resolvent set, denoted $\rho(A)$, is defined as follows:
$$
\rho(A) = \{z \in \mathbb C \mid (zI-A)^{-1} \text{ exists }\}
$$
