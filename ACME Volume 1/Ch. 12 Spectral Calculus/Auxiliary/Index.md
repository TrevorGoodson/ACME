## Technical Details
###### Assumptions
$B$ is a square matrix
$k \in \mathbb N$
$\mathscr N$ is the [[Kernel (Null Space)]]
###### Definition
The index of $B$, denoted $\text{ind}(B)$, is defined as the smallest value for $k$ that satisfies the following:
$$
\mathscr N(B^k) = \mathscr N(B^{k+1})
$$
