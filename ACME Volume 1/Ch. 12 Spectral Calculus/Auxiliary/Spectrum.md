#Definition Definition 12.3.1
## Intuition

## Technical Details
###### Assumptions
$A \in M_n(\mathbb F)$
$\rho(A)$ is the [[Resolvent Set]] of $A$

###### Definition
The spectrum of $A$, denoted $\sigma(A)$, is defined to be the compliment of the resolvent set of $A$; $\sigma(A) = \mathbb C \setminus \rho(A)$.

## Key Results
- It is non-empty (Corollary 12.3.12)
