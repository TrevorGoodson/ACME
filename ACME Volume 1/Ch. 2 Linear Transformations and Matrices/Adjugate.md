## Technical Details
###### Assumptions
$A \in M_n(\mathbb F)$
$c_{ij}$ is the element in $A$ at the $i$th row and $j$th column
$M_{ij}$ is the [[Determinant]] of the matrix derived by removing the $i$th row and $j$th column of $A$
###### Definition
The adjugate of $A$, denoted $\text{adj}(A)$, is a square matrix of size $n$ where each element is defined by
$$
c_{ij} = (-1)^{i+j}M_{ij}
$$
