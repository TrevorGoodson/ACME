## Technical Details
###### Assumptions
$\cdot ^{-1}$ is the [[Inverse]]
$U,V \subset \mathbb R^n$
$U,V$ are [[Open]]
$\Psi : U \to V$ is the candidate for being a diffeomorphism
$\Psi$ is $C^1$ ([[Continuously Differentiable|C-k]]) and [[Bijective]]
###### Definition
$\Psi$ is defined to be a diffeomorphism if $\Psi^{-1}$ is $C^1$
## Key Results
- The composition of two diffeomorphisms is a diffeomorphism (Remark 8.7.2)
