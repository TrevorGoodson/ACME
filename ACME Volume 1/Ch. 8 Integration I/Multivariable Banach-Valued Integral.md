#Theorem Theorem 8.1.9

## Technical Definition
$X$ is a [[Banach Space]]
$n \in \mathbb N$
$a, b \in \mathbb R^n$
$S([a, b]; X)$ is the set of all [[Step Function|step functions]] $[a, b] \to X$
$\overline{S([a, b]; X)}$ is the [[Closure]] of $S([a, b]; X)$ (see [[Regulated-Integrable Function]])
Note $\overline{S([a, b]; X)} \subset L^{\infty}([a,b];X)$ ([[n-Interval#Step Function|Proposition 8.1.5]], see [[L-p Space]])
$\mathscr I : S([a,b]; X) \to X$
