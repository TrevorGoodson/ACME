#Theorem Proposition 8.1.8
## Technical Details
###### Assumptions
$[a,b] \subset \mathbb R^n$ ([[n-Interval]])
$X$ is a [[Banach Space]]
$S([a,b];X)$ is the set of all [[Step Function|step functions]] from $[a,b]$ to $X$
$\mathscr I : S([a,b];X) \to X$ ([[Integral of a Step Function]])
$\lambda$ is the [[Measure]] on $\mathbb R^n$
###### Conclusions
$\mathscr I$ is a [[Bounded Linear Transformation]]
$\lambda ([a,b])$ is the [[Norm#Norm of a Linear Transformation|norm]] of $\mathscr I$
